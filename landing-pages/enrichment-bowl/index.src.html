Why it's different

It's Not Just Another Flat Lick Mat

The flat-mat format is everywhere. This bowl changes the shape of the activity: height, angles, depth, and movement.

Traditional flat mat

  • Flat interaction, one angle
  • Basic surface texture
  • A commonly duplicated format
  • Often slides or lifts at the edges

Elevated interactive bowl

  • Elevated form your dog works around
  • Varied licking angles
  • Deeper, varied textures
  • Wobble and movement as they lick
  • A visibly more interactive experience

    Questions

    Before you decide

    What can I put on the bowl?

    Use pet-appropriate spreads, wet food, treats, or other suitable foods recommended for your pet.

    Can I freeze it?

    The target specification is designed for freeze-friendly use. Final material documentation should be confirmed before launch.

    Replace with confirmed material documentation.
    Does it stick to every surface?

    No. It is designed to work best on clean, smooth surfaces. Suction effectiveness varies by surface texture.

    Is this a chew toy?

    No. This is an enrichment feeding product and should be used under supervision.

    Is it dishwasher safe?

    Final cleaning instructions depend on the approved production model.

    Replace once dishwasher status is confirmed.
    What size dogs can use it?

    Final sizing guidelines will be confirmed after sample approval.

    Replace with sizing by dog weight.
    How long will it keep my dog occupied?

    Engagement time varies depending on the dog, the food used, the amount, the texture, and whether the product is frozen.
