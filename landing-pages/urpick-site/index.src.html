Why UrPick

Simple things, done properly.

Most pet products are made for a category. Ours are made for a moment: the bath, the brush, the nail trim, the long afternoon. That changes what we leave out.

  • Routine first

    Designed around what happens every week, not what sells on a shelf.

  • Dogs and cats

    Different animals, different habits. One idea is not shrunk to fit the other.

  • Nothing unnecessary

    Fewer parts, simpler care, less to go wrong.

  • Honest claims

    If we haven't tested it, we won't say it.

About us

Everyday essentials for pets and the people who love them.

UrPick is a new pet-care brand making thoughtfully designed essentials for the routines you and your pets share: bath time, grooming, treat time, and the quiet afternoons in between.

Built for routines. We start with how a moment actually goes at home, not with a product category. Baths, brushing, nail trims, treat time: the things that happen every week.

Dogs and cats, both. Different animals, different habits. We design for each rather than shrinking one idea to fit the other, and we're listening to households with other pets too.

Nothing unnecessary. Fewer parts, simpler care, less to go wrong. Nothing on a product that isn't doing a job.

Honest by design. Only the claims we can stand behind. If we haven't tested it, we won't say it.

Our first product is in development now. Details when they're real.
