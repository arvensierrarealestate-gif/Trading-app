Why UrPick

Simple things, done properly.

Most pet products are made for a category. Ours are made for a moment: the bath, the brush, the nail trim, the long afternoon. That changes what we leave out.

  • Routine first

    Designed around what happens every week, not what sells on a shelf.

  • Dogs and cats

    Different animals, different habits. One idea is not shrunk to fit the other.

  • Nothing unnecessary

    Fewer parts, simpler care, less to go wrong.

  • Honest claims

    If we haven't tested it, we won't say it.

        Shipping & Returns

        How orders ship and how returns work.

        We are not taking orders yet, so this page describes the shipping and return experience we intend to offer when UrPick launches.

        Draft for review. This policy is still being finalized and may change before launch.

        Last updated: October 13, 2026

        Where We Ship

        At launch, we plan to ship throughout the United States, including Washington, D.C.

        Shipping availability and rates for Alaska, Hawaii, U.S. territories, APO/FPO addresses, and P.O. boxes may vary depending on carrier availability and will be shown at checkout when available.

        We do not plan to offer international shipping at launch.

        If you live outside the United States and are interested in ordering from us, contact us at yourpick2025@gmail.com. International shipping may be added in the future.

        Processing Time

        Orders are expected to be processed within 1–2 business days, Monday through Friday, excluding U.S. federal holidays.

        During holidays, product launches, promotional periods, or unusually high order volume, processing may take slightly longer.

        Once your order ships, you will receive a shipping confirmation email with tracking information.

        Please allow up to 24–48 hours for tracking information to begin updating after a shipping label is created.

        Delivery Time & Shipping Cost

        Our intended launch shipping structure is:

        • Standard Shipping: approximately 3–7 business days
        • Orders of $55 or more: Free standard shipping
        • Orders under $55: $4.99 standard shipping

        Available shipping options, estimated delivery times, and costs will always be displayed before checkout is completed.

        Delivery estimates begin after an order has been processed and handed to the carrier.

        Delivery dates are estimates and cannot be guaranteed. Weather, carrier disruptions, incorrect addresses, holiday volume, and other circumstances outside our control may cause delays.

        Free Shipping

        Free standard shipping is planned for qualifying U.S. orders of $55 or more after discounts and before applicable taxes.

        Orders below the free-shipping threshold will be charged the standard shipping rate shown at checkout.

        Address Changes

        Please review your shipping address carefully before placing an order.

        If you discover an error, contact us at yourpick2025@gmail.com as soon as possible with your order number and the correct address.

        We will make every reasonable effort to update an address before the order ships, but changes cannot be guaranteed once an order has entered fulfillment or has been transferred to the carrier.

        Tracking Your Order

        Once your order ships, you will receive an email containing your tracking information.

        Carrier tracking may take 24–48 hours to update after shipment.

        If your tracking has not updated for an extended period or your package appears delayed, contact us at yourpick2025@gmail.com and we will help review the shipment.

        Lost or Missing Packages

        If tracking shows that your order was delivered but you cannot locate it, please first:

        • Check around your delivery location
        • Ask household members
        • Check with neighbors, building staff, or your front desk
        • Review any delivery photo or carrier notification
        • Contact the shipping carrier

        If the package still cannot be located, email yourpick2025@gmail.com with your order number and tracking information.

        Lost-package situations will be reviewed individually based on the available carrier information.

        Duties & Taxes

        Because we plan to ship only within the United States at launch, international customs duties should not apply.

        Applicable sales tax will be calculated at checkout where required.

        If international shipping is introduced in the future, applicable customs duties, import taxes, VAT, or other local charges will be explained as part of the international shipping policy.

        Returns & Refunds

        We want you to feel confident ordering from UrPick.

        Eligible items may be returned within 30 days of delivery.

        For a standard return, the item must generally be:

        • Unused
        • Unopened
        • In its original packaging
        • In resalable condition

        To request a return, email yourpick2025@gmail.com with:

        • Your name
        • Order number
        • Item you would like to return
        • Reason for the return

        Please contact us before sending anything back so we can provide the correct return instructions.

        Return Shipping

        For standard returns involving a change of mind, preference, sizing, color selection, or similar reasons, the customer is responsible for return shipping.

        If we sent the wrong item, or your product arrived damaged or defective, you will not be responsible for the cost of correcting the issue.

        Depending on the situation, we may provide:

        • A replacement
        • A prepaid return label
        • A full refund
        • Another appropriate resolution

        Damaged, Defective or Incorrect Items

        If your order arrives damaged, defective, or incorrect, contact us within 7 days of delivery at yourpick2025@gmail.com.

        Please include:

        • Your order number
        • A brief description of the issue
        • Clear photos or video showing the problem when possible

        Once reviewed, we will arrange an appropriate replacement or refund at no additional cost to you.

        Consumable & Hygiene-Sensitive Products

        For health, sanitation, and safety reasons, opened or used consumable or hygiene-sensitive products generally cannot be returned for resale.

        This may include products such as:

        • Dog diapers
        • Pee pads
        • Disposable pet-care products
        • Grooming or cleaning products
        • Other products that cannot safely be resold once opened or used

        This restriction does not prevent us from helping if an item arrives damaged, defective, incorrect, or there is another legitimate product-quality issue.

        Refunds

        Once an approved return has been received and inspected, we will notify you regarding the status of your refund.

        Approved refunds will be issued to the original payment method within approximately 5–10 business days after the return is received and approved.

        Your bank or card issuer may require additional processing time before the refund appears in your account.

        Original shipping charges are generally non-refundable unless the return resulted from an error on our part or the item arrived damaged or defective.

        Order Cancellations

        You may request cancellation before your order has shipped.

        Because orders can move into fulfillment quickly, cancellation cannot be guaranteed after processing has begun.

        Once an order has shipped, it cannot be canceled and must follow the applicable return process.

        Personalized Products

        If UrPick introduces customized or personalized products, those items may not qualify for standard returns because they cannot normally be resold.

        If we make an error in the personalization, or the item arrives damaged or defective, we will replace or refund it as appropriate.

        Final Sale Items

        Certain clearance, promotional, customized, or specially marked products may be designated Final Sale.

        If an item is Final Sale, that designation will be clearly disclosed before purchase.

        Final Sale restrictions will not prevent us from addressing products that arrive damaged, defective, or incorrect.

        Exchanges

        At launch, we do not plan to offer direct exchanges.

        If an eligible item needs to be replaced because of size, color, or preference, the customer may return the original item according to this policy and place a new order for the preferred item.

        This helps us process inventory, refunds, and replacement orders more accurately.

        Fair Use

        Our return policy is designed to protect genuine customers while allowing us to operate responsibly.

        We reserve the right to review or decline return or refund requests involving suspected fraud, abuse, repeated misuse of the return policy, or products returned in a condition inconsistent with the stated reason for return.

        This provision does not affect legitimate claims involving damaged, defective, or incorrect products.

        Questions?

        For questions about shipping, tracking, returns, damaged products, or refunds, contact us at yourpick2025@gmail.com.

        Please include your order number whenever possible so we can assist you more quickly.

        About us

        Made for real life with the pets who are already family.

        Pets bring a lot into our lives: companionship, personality, comfort, laughter, and occasionally a mess we definitely did not plan for.

        UrPick was created around a simple idea: the everyday parts of living with pets should be easier.

        We look for practical products that solve real problems pet parents deal with, whether that means making a routine simpler, keeping the home cleaner, making walks more comfortable, or helping everyday care feel a little less complicated.

        We are not trying to fill a store with everything made for pets.

        We want to find the things that are actually worth bringing into your home.
