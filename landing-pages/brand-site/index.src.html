What we're about

Designed Around the Moments You Actually Have

Not more stuff. A few things that make the ordinary parts of pet care easier, calmer, and a little more fun.

Built for routines

Baths, brushing, nail trims, treat time. We start from the moments that happen every week, not from a product category.

Dogs and cats, both

Different animals, different habits. We design for each rather than shrinking one idea to fit the other.

Honest by design

Fewer parts, simpler care, and only the claims we can stand behind. If we haven't tested it, we won't say it.

Our approach

Simple Things, Done Properly

Start with the routineWe watch how the moment actually goes before we decide what to make.
Leave out what isn't neededFewer pieces to lose, clean, or chew. Nothing on the product that isn't doing a job.
Say only what we've testedNo miracle language. What a product does, in plain words, after we've used it ourselves.
