Questions

Before you decide

Does the fountain need to stay plugged in?

No. The planned product is rechargeable and designed for cordless operation. You charge it over USB-C, then place it wherever your cat drinks.

What size is the reservoir?

Our selected target specification is approximately 3.0–3.2 liters.

Our target production specification requires SUS304 stainless steel for the drinking surface. Final components will be confirmed after supplier validation.
